# AI corrections log

A short record of the times the AI coding assistant got something wrong and a human caught it. One entry per mistake, newest at the bottom. The log is written by the developer, not by the assistant.

Why keep it: it shows where human review made a difference, and it feeds the rules in `AGENTS.md`.

## Entry template

Copy this block, fill it in, and add it under "Entries".

```
### YYYY-MM-DD, Step <id>: <short title>
- **What the assistant did:**
- **What was wrong:**
- **How it was caught:** (diff reading, a test, the checks, CI, the review comment, running it)
- **Fix:**
- **Rule or lesson:** (a line added to AGENTS.md, a new test, or "none")
```
